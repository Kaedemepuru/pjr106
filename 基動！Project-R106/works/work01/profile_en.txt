This work was inspired by observing the online media consumed by my younger brother’s generation. I noticed that a significant portion of the content they encounter consists of memes, parodies, and reinterpretations of familiar cartoon characters. This led me to reflect on how different generations communicate through media and how internet culture reshapes our shared visual language.

With the rise of meme culture, many of the cartoon characters that defined my childhood have gradually taken on new meanings. Once associated with innocence and nostalgia, they have been transformed into humorous, absurd, and often ironic cultural symbols through endless online circulation and remixing.

Through Teletubbies, I explore the relationship between memes and childhood memories, examining how digital culture reconstructs familiar characters and how these transformed images become a new medium of communication between generations.
