Rooted in my childhood home in Puzi, Chiayi, the project begins with a violent typhoon that left the roof in ruins, compelling me to re-examine the bond between myself, the physical house, and my lineage. Combining 3D scans of the structure with handcrafted origami objects, the narrative imagines my grandparents' lives back in 1970 while echoing the sensory memories of returning home in adulthood.
